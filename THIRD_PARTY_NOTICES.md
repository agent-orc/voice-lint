# Third-party notices

No third-party binary, style pack, model, dataset, or source code is currently distributed by this design-only repository.

Research references do not make their projects dependencies. Before a release bundles or redistributes any third-party asset, this file must record:

- component and upstream URL;
- exact version or immutable revision;
- license and copyright notice;
- what Voice Lint distributes or adapts;
- required license text, attribution, source offer, or modification notice;
- integrity hash and where corresponding source is available.

Known candidates under evaluation include Vale (MIT), LanguageTool (LGPL-2.1-or-later), Harper (Apache-2.0), textlint (MIT), and separately licensed rule packs or model assets. Their presence in research documentation does not imply that they are bundled.

Separate-process integration does not remove obligations triggered by distribution. Consult the upstream license and obtain legal review where needed.
